# Sanskar Public School — Website Content & Build Brief

Use this as the prompt/spec document for Lovable. It covers brand direction, site structure, and full written content for every page.

---

## 1. Brand Direction

**Site name:** Sanskar Public School (umbrella site also covering Sanskar Little Winners, Sanskar Talent Academy, and the on-campus Sports Arena)

**Color palette**
- Deep Teal (primary): `#146B64` — headers, nav, primary buttons
- Deep Teal Dark (backgrounds/hero): `#0B3D3A`
- Coral (accent / CTA): `#FF6B54`
- Gold (secondary accent): `#E8A33D`
- Cream (page background): `#FBF7F0`
- Ink (body text): `#22282A`

**Typography**
- Display/headings: Fraunces (serif, warm, editorial)
- Body/UI: Inter (sans-serif)

**Tone of voice:** warm, trustworthy, rooted in values but modern — parents should feel this is a well-run, professionally managed school, not a generic template site.

**Recurring visual motif:** a "weekly rhythm" strip (Mon–Sat) marking Club Days vs Foundation Days — reuse this small graphic across the Homepage and Academics page as a wayfinding device.
