# Mathura school search research — 23 September 2026

## Findings and implementation

A search for “best school in Mathura” and “top 10 schools in Mathura” returned school directories, comparison articles and Sanskar's existing homepage and shortlist. This is a qualitative search snapshot, not a verified Google rank report or search-volume estimate. Results vary by location and time.

The comparison intent is clear: parents need school locations, boards, fees, admission details and evidence they can check. Keep the homepage focused on Sanskar and the existing comparison URL focused on local school options. Avoid competing duplicate keyword pages.

Changes in this update:
- Preserve the homepage title targeting “Best School in Mathura” and the existing “top 10 schools in Mathura” article URL.
- Add the best-school phrase to the parent guide H1 and a concrete comparison checklist.
- Retain the shortlist's disclosure that it is the school's editorial selection, not an independent ranking.
- Add an original educational article about reading habits for Mathura families, with realistic home activities and campus-visit questions.
- Link the new article from the homepage, list it in the blog and register it in the sitemap and server-rendered routes.
- Format publication dates from article data and use a classic Georgia serif for H1 headings without an additional font download.

## Follow-up requiring account access or school information

1. Use Google Search Console to establish the actual impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for both target phrases. Inspect the updated URLs and submit the sitemap at https://sanskarschools.com/sitemap.xml.
2. Keep the verified Google Business Profile name, address, phone, opening hours and campus photos accurate. Request honest reviews from families without incentives.
3. Publish the school's approved current fee information, admission dates and transport details when available. Do not invent prices, routes, awards or performance statistics.
4. Review query and enquiry performance monthly. Expand content around real parent questions rather than repeating keywords.

Google's local results consider relevance, distance and prominence. Website changes can improve relevance and usability but cannot guarantee first place. Search Console and Business Profile changes were not performed in this update.
